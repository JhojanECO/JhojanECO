PID Line Follower Lab

Tune the controller of a differential-drive line follower and watch what each gain does to the left and right motor PWM (0 to 255). The robot reads the tape with an 8-sensor array, the same way a QTR-8 does: position runs from 0 to 7000, and the error is position − 3500.
